Strategic Insights Executive Summary
📦 Order Performance — March 2026
  • 175 orders placed generating ₹3,92,813 GMV. Peak days: March 8–9 with 13 orders each — likely ad campaign days.
  • Cancellation rate 30.9% — 31 of 54 canceled were COD orders. 23 were prepaid suggesting product/expectation mismatch.
  • 60.3% delivery rate of shipped — 73 delivered from 121 shipped. Target should be 75%+ for healthy D2C operation.
  • Self-fulfilled 5 orders (₹9,545) likely influencer sends or pop-up store sales — good sign of offline traction.
🚨 RTO Crisis — Immediate Action Needed
  • 29.8% RTO rate of shipped orders — 2x industry average of 12–15%. This is the #1 business risk.
  • 100% of RTO are COD orders — Zero prepaid orders came back. COD = RTO risk. Prepaid = safe.
  • RTO states: J&K, Arunachal Pradesh, Bihar — restrict COD for 40+ high-risk pincodes in these states.
  • ₹30,586 total loss from RTO (freight + tied-up product cost). This can be recovered with better COD screening.
💰 P&L — Financial Health
  • Net P&L: ₹26,874 on ₹1.76L realized revenue = 15.3% net margin. Healthy but can be improved significantly.
  • ROAS 8.45x on GMV but real ROAS 3.78x on delivered revenue — this is the honest number to optimize.
  • If RTO drops from 30% to 15%, net profit increases by ~₹15,000 (freight saved + additional revenue realized).
  • Product margin per delivered unit: ₹1,499 average price – ₹500 cost = ₹999 gross per unit (67% gross margin).
✅ Growth Opportunities
  • South India (KA+TS+KL+TN) = 39 orders with better delivery rates — scale Meta ads here aggressively.
  • Black Signature Gurkha Pants is the hero SKU — runs across all size variants. Focus on stocking 30–36 range.
  • Prepaid conversion — only 13.1% orders are prepaid. Offer 5% discount on prepaid to push this to 25%+.
  • AOV opportunity: 80% orders are 1 product. Bundle deals (Pant + Shirt) can push AOV to ₹3,200+.
